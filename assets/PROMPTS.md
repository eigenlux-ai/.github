# Eigenlux artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The avatar uses the banner as its visual style reference. Original PNGs are retained without post-processing.

## Banner

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: GitHub organization hero banner for Eigenlux.
Create a panoramic 3:1 brand banner, approximately 1800 x 600. Concept: "a light of your own" — a small steady light making a vast dark space intelligible. A philosophical personal-computing project, quiet, contemplative, precise; East Asian empty-space aesthetics meeting optical design.
Background deep ink charcoal almost black, subtle texture. Left 54% spacious typography: exact large word "Eigenlux" in warm moon-white, refined modern humanist sans-serif, beautiful confident kerning. Under it exactly "A light of your own." in smaller type. Nothing else written.
Right 40%: ONE iconic sculptural optical object, a thick upright circular dark graphite disc with a vertical narrow opening through its center, like two precise crescent forms parted slightly. Warm ivory light shines from the narrow vertical aperture, producing a delicate pool of light below and faint clean rays in the surrounding darkness. Edges are polished pearl-silver glass, with extremely restrained pale amber highlights. The silhouette should suggest both an opening eye and a lamp, abstract rather than literal. Studio-quality material and illumination, meditative elegant minimal composition. One point of insight illuminates the darkness. Broad negative space, subtle smoky volumetric light only adjacent to aperture, no busy particles.
Do not use mint green, neon gradients, rainbow, gold phoenix, robot, brain, atom symbols, stars, text panels, code UI, badges or fantasy imagery. No promises of consciousness, no science diagrams. Full bleed rectangular banner with safe 6% text inset, no border or watermark. All text is exact and readable.
```

## Avatar

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square GitHub organization avatar for Eigenlux.
Use the attached banner as a style reference. Create a single compact brand icon on deep ink charcoal, square 1024x1024. Center one perfectly circular graphite-and-pearl optical disc, face-on, occupying 65% of image width with generous safe margins for circular avatar crops. The disc consists of two smooth dark semicircular forms parted by one very narrow straight vertical opening of warm ivory light. A fine pearl-silver outer rim catches light. The straight luminous line is geometric and restrained, NOT shaped like a pupil or eye, no eye imagery. Simplify the banner sculpture into an elegant highly legible modern emblem. Very subtle soft glow, minimal surface grain, no floor scene, no mist, no rays, no objects around it. Palette charcoal, moon-white, very faint champagne warmth. Strong silhouette at 48px. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no border, no extra decorations.
```
